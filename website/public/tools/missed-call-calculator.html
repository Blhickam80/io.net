Why this happens

Most local service businesses (contractors, dentists, salons, auto shops, real estate teams) can't answer every call while they're on a job, with a patient, or after hours. Callers who don't get an answer frequently move on to the next result in a search or a competitor's ad rather than leaving a voicemail — the lead is gone before you even know it existed.

How businesses fix this

The two most common fixes are (1) hiring more front-desk staff, which is expensive and doesn't cover nights/weekends, or (2) an AI phone answering / AI receptionist service that answers every call 24/7, qualifies the caller, and books the appointment automatically.
