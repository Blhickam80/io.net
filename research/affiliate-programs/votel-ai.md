# Votel.ai — Raw Research (Agent Report, 2026-08-27)

Source: general-purpose research agent, WebSearch + WebFetch against votel.ai, app.votel.ai/business-referral-program, Capterra, and affiliate directories (zPlatform.ai, Rewardful, PartnerStack, Lasso).

## 1. Product
Votel is a white-label "AI agency platform" (Atmeon LLC), not a standalone AI-receptionist product. It bundles a website/funnel builder, CRM, AI Assistant (prompt-to-build), an AI voice/chat "Agent" (answers calls/messages, qualifies leads, books appointments), and an "AI Worker" for backend follow-up/CRM automation — pitched as replacing ~38 point tools. Feature naming (sub-accounts, SaaS Mode, rebilling, white-label) is identical to GoHighLevel's terminology, strongly suggesting Votel is a GoHighLevel-based reseller/skin rather than a ground-up build; this could not be confirmed directly (no page states it explicitly). Primary buyer is marketing/local-service agencies who resell it to SMB clients under their own brand; a solo-operator ($97/mo) tier also lets an individual use it directly. Source: https://votel.ai/

## 2. Pricing
- Free tier: $0/mo, limited (API calls, 60 integrations, MMS/US, call history) — Capterra listing.
- Pro: $97/mo (5 sub-accounts, unlimited agents/contacts/users, 300 AI "MCP tools," 19 languages) — listed as discounted from $497.
- Unlimited Agency Pro (white label): $297/mo (discounted from $997) — unlimited sub-accounts, rebilling, SaaS Mode, white-label branding.
- Usage add-ons: AI credits $0.25 each; voice calling from $0.01-$0.039/min; STT $0.009/min; phone/SMS/email billed as consumed.
No annual pricing or enterprise tier surfaced. Sources: https://votel.ai/pricing, https://www.capterra.com/p/10053242/Votel-ai/pricing/

## 5. Conversion Funnel

Sales cycle appears short/self-serve for the $97 tier; agency-scale ($297 white-label) deals likely involve a sales call.

## 6. Competitive Landscape
Votel sits at the intersection of two categories:
- AI voice/receptionist point solutions: Smith.ai, Ruby Receptionist, My AI Front Desk, Goodcall, Numa/Rosie, Bland AI, Synthflow, Vapi, Retell AI, air.ai, Dialzara — narrower, often cheaper at entry ($20-$100/mo), more specialized/mature in voice quality; several (Bland, Synthflow, Vapi, Retell) are developer-first platforms rather than SMB-turnkey.
- All-in-one agency/CRM platforms: GoHighLevel (the likely underlying platform Votel resembles or resells), Vendasta, Kartra — GoHighLevel has a well-established, heavily-marketed affiliate program (widely reported ~40% recurring) and dwarfs Votel in reviews/market presence.
Votel's positioning is "cheaper all-in-one bundle" ($97 vs. $2,792 of point tools) rather than best-in-class voice quality; no independent benchmark comparing its call quality to Bland/Retell/Synthflow was found.
