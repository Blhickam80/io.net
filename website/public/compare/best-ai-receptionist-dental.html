Why this matters for dental practices specifically

A new-patient call for a dental practice is worth far more than a single visit — it's the start of an ongoing relationship (cleanings, exams, treatment plans) worth potentially thousands of dollars over time. Missed new-patient calls during busy chair-side hours are a direct hit to practice growth, not just a single missed appointment. Use our missed call revenue calculator with your average patient value to estimate your own number.

What to look for

  • Appointment scheduling integration — booking directly into your practice management software (or at minimum collecting preferred times) avoids phone tag.
  • New-patient vs. existing-patient handling — new patients often need different qualifying questions (insurance, reason for visit) than existing patients calling to reschedule.
  • After-hours coverage — many patients call about pain or emergencies outside business hours.
  • HIPAA-conscious workflows — ask any AI receptionist vendor directly how they handle patient information before sending any PHI through the system.
