Why this matters for roofing specifically

Roofing leads are often storm-driven and time-sensitive — a homeowner with a leak calling multiple companies in the same hour. A missed call doesn't just lose a job; with an average re-roof or repair ticket often running well into four figures, a handful of missed calls a month can represent real, quantifiable revenue. Use our missed call revenue calculator with your own call volume and average ticket to see the number for your business.

What to look for

  • 24/7 coverage — storm calls come in nights and weekends, not just business hours.
  • Appointment booking / lead qualification — capturing address, damage type, and insurance-claim status upfront saves the estimator time.
  • SMS follow-up — many callers prefer a text confirmation over waiting on hold.
  • Simple setup — most roofing companies don't have in-house IT; look for self-serve onboarding.
