# Short 03 — "How AI phone answering actually works (60 seconds)"

**Format:** Talking-to-camera OR screen recording narrating a generic call flow (no specific vendor UI required — describe the concept, don't demo a product you haven't personally tested). Once you have hands-on access to a vendor's demo/trial, this script can be upgraded to a real screen-recording demo — see note at bottom.
**Length target:** 40-50 seconds
**Goal:** Educational/explainer hook for people who've heard the term "AI receptionist" but don't actually know what it does — wider top-of-funnel than Shorts 01/02, sets up credibility for the comparison content.

## Script

**[0:00-0:04] HOOK — on screen text: "What does an AI receptionist actually DO?"**
> You've probably seen "AI receptionist" or "AI phone answering" somewhere and wondered what it actually does. Here's the 60-second version.

**[0:04-0:20] BODY — the mechanism, step by step**
> When someone calls your business, instead of ringing out to voicemail, an AI system picks up and has an actual conversation — sounds like a real person, not a robotic phone tree. It answers basic questions about your business, asks what the caller needs, and for a lot of these tools, it can actually book the appointment directly into your calendar.

**[0:20-0:32] BODY — the outcome**
> So instead of losing that call to voicemail — or to your competitor — you get a booked appointment, or at minimum a text with the caller's info, while you're still on the job.

**[0:32-0:42] CREDIBILITY / HONESTY BEAT**
> Not every one of these tools works the same way, and pricing varies a lot — I put together a comparison of the ones actually worth looking at.

**[0:42-0:48] CTA — on screen text: link in description**
> Link's in the description if you want the full breakdown.
