# SEO_PLAN.md

## Money keyword clusters (seed list — expand in seo/keywords.csv)
- `best ai receptionist for [roofing|dental|real estate|hvac|plumbing|law firm]`
- `[product] pricing` / `[product] cost`
- `[product] vs [competitor]` (e.g., "gohighlevel vs votel", "gohighlevel alternatives")
- `[product] alternatives`
- `[product] review`
- `missed call revenue calculator` / `how much do missed calls cost a business`
- `ai receptionist for small business`
- `best crm for [home service|contractors|real estate agents]`
- `how much does an ai receptionist cost`

## Programmatic SEO rules (guardrails against thin content)
- A `{category} for {industry}` page ships only once it has: an industry-specific pain-point intro, at least 3 genuinely compared options with pricing, a relevant calculator/CTA, and unique FAQ content — not a templated mail-merge.
- Every page gets JSON-LD (Article, Review, or FAQPage as applicable).
- Internal linking: every new money page links to/from the relevant free tool and at least one other money page.

## AEO/GEO (AI search visibility)
- Structured, direct-answer paragraphs near the top of each page (definition/answer in the first 2-3 sentences) so AI answer engines can extract them cleanly.
- FAQPage schema on comparison/review pages.
- Clear, factual, source-cited claims (helps both AI-search trust and E-E-A-T).

## Backlinks (no budget)
- None purchased. Organic link earning via: genuinely useful free tools (naturally linkable), guest contributions to relevant communities/newsletters once we have content to point to, and directory listings for legitimate free tool directories (e.g., free SaaS tool aggregators) — track attempts in `outreach/`.

## Tracking
- `seo/keywords.csv` — keyword, cluster, target page, search intent, status (planned/drafted/published/ranking)
- Rankings tracked manually via Search Console once available (ACCESS_NEEDED #4); no paid rank tracker.
