Why response time matters

A lead who just filled out a form or called your business is actively comparing options right now, often with a browser tab open to a competitor. The longer the gap before you respond, the more likely they've already booked with someone else, lost urgency, or simply moved on. This effect is well documented in sales research generally — the exact size of the effect varies a lot by industry, lead source, and how "warm" the lead was to begin with, which is why this calculator asks you to enter your own improvement assumption rather than assuming one for you.

How businesses close the gap

The two common approaches are (1) staffing someone to monitor and respond to leads immediately, which is expensive and hard to sustain outside business hours, or (2) automated first-response tools — an AI receptionist that picks up calls instantly, or a CRM with instant lead-routing and auto-response for web form submissions — that close the gap without requiring a human to be watching every channel at all times.

FAQ

What is a good lead response time? Responding within 5 minutes is a commonly cited benchmark for maximizing close rate, since that's roughly the window before a lead's interest and urgency start to drop off. What matters most for your business is how your current response time compares to your current close rate — which is exactly what this calculator uses, not a generic industry claim.
