## Channel prioritization (per STRATEGY.md 80/20 model)
**Channel 1 — LIVE:** YouTube — **https://www.youtube.com/@yardwick** (created 2026-08-27). Start posting Shorts immediately rather than waiting on long-form production — YouTube Shorts has cold-start algorithmic distribution, so a brand-new channel can still get real view counts on a good hook, unlike platforms that gate reach behind an existing follower count. Long-form video (screen-recording demos) is the durable/compounding play once cadence is established, but isn't the entry point.

**Channel 2 — next, near-zero extra cost:** TikTok. Once a Short exists, repost the identical file — same production cost, same cold-start algorithmic reach dynamic. Not "once #1 shows traction" — do it right after the first Short is up, since the marginal cost is ~0.

**Deprioritized until #1/#2 show traction:** Instagram, X/LinkedIn, Facebook, Pinterest — reach on these leans much harder on existing network/follower count, and buyer-intent fit for this audience is weaker. Don't create these accounts yet.

**Deprioritized for now:** Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram static posts, X/LinkedIn organic-only (low buyer-intent match for this audience without an existing personal brand to leverage) — revisit if/when a specific experiment shows promise (log in EXPERIMENTS.md, don't add channels by default).
