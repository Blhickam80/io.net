Why this adds up faster than it feels like it should

A single no-show doesn't feel like a big loss in the moment. But multiply a modest no-show rate across hundreds of monthly appointments, and most of those slots go completely unfilled because there usually isn't enough notice to call someone in from a waitlist. For dental practices, med spas, and salons specifically, this is often one of the largest controllable revenue leaks in the business — bigger than most owners estimate until they run the actual numbers.

How businesses reduce no-shows

The most effective fixes are usually automated: reminder texts/emails sent a day and an hour before the appointment, easy self-service rescheduling links (so a patient reschedules instead of just not showing up), and two-way texting so front-desk staff can quickly fill a cancellation from a waitlist.
