Best AI Receptionist for Real Estate Agents

A buyer calling about a listing rarely waits around. If you're mid-showing or at another appointment, that lead is calling the next agent on the search results page within minutes. Here's how AI receptionist services address that for solo agents and small teams.

Why this matters for real estate specifically

Real estate is one of the few local-service categories where a single missed call can represent a five- or six-figure commission, not just a job ticket. Buyer and seller leads are also unusually time-sensitive — most agents report the highest conversion odds are in the first few minutes after initial contact, which is exactly when an agent showing a property can't pick up. Use our missed call revenue calculator with your own numbers (commission per closed deal instead of a job ticket) to see what this is worth for your business.

What to look for

  • Lead qualification, not just message-taking — capturing property address/MLS number, budget range, and pre-approval status upfront saves the agent a callback just to ask basic questions.
  • Showing scheduling — the ability to offer available showing slots directly instead of a multi-text back-and-forth.
  • CRM handoff — the lead needs to land somewhere you'll actually see it (see the stack section below), not just a voicemail transcript.
  • After-hours coverage — a large share of buyer browsing and calling happens evenings and weekends, exactly when agents are with other clients.
