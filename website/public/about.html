About & Methodology

How we research, and how we're funded.

How we research

We build comparisons from publicly available information: vendor pricing pages, documented feature sets, public reviews (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit), and affiliate/partner program terms. When we've personally tested a product, we say so explicitly and describe what we did. We do not claim hands-on testing we haven't done.

How we're funded

This site is supported by affiliate commissions: when you sign up for certain products through links on this site, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never determines whether we cover a product, but it is a financial relationship you should know about, per FTC guidelines.

Corrections

Software pricing and features change often. If you spot outdated information, it's an oversight, not intentional — content is reviewed and updated on an ongoing basis.
